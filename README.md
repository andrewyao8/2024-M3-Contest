# 2024-M3-Contest
Honorable Mention Paper from the 2024 M3 Mathworks Math Modeling Contest.

The "M3 MathWorks Math Modeling Challenge" is a concurrent 14 hour competition where high school students work in teams to solve real-world problems using applied mathematics, sponsored by MathWorks and organized by the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM).
